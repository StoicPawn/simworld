## M11: spatial life

`PresenceLedger` records current positions and historical activity per cell: visits, unique visitors, encounters and productive uses. People move locally through passable cells using bounded stochastic choice influenced by fertility, timber, habitability, movement cost, accumulated familiarity and individual variation.

Co-presence produces explicit encounters. Previously unrelated people can acquire acquaintance or rivalry ties only after actual spatial contact in this new pathway.

`site_intensity` is a derived analytical view. It can tell us that a beach, bend, crossing or valley floor has become heavily used without declaring it a market, centre or strategic location.

## Core simplification rules

- high-level social categories should be derived whenever possible;
- asset != possession != use != control != claim != recognized claim != formal property;
- market/centre/route/settlement/house/state are not primitive merely because humans have names for them;
- site importance must emerge from physical affordances and accumulated activity;
- site intensity may affect observation/refinement, but must not become causal popularity by itself;
- world truth != actor knowledge;
- need/shock != prescribed action;
- kinship != household != loyalty != political identity;
- organization != state;
- authority != legitimacy;
- never tune target counts of historical events.

## Complexity budget

Before adding a primitive, ask whether the phenomenon can instead be represented as state, relation, process, observation, accumulated history or derived view. Prefer the smaller representation.
